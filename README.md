# Netflix_project
Netflix Content Analysis — Key Findings
========================================
1. Content Type: ~63.5% of Netflix content is Movies vs ~36.5% 
   TV Shows, indicating Netflix positions itself primarily as 
   a movie platform, though TV Shows are growing.

2. Peak Growth Year: Netflix added the most content in 2019,
   with a noticeable drop in 2020/2021, likely due to COVID-19 
   halting global film and TV production.

3. Target Audience: TV-MA is the most common rating, suggesting 
   Netflix primarily targets adults (18+). However, the presence 
   of family-friendly ratings shows a multi-demographic strategy.

4. Top Genre: Drama dominates Netflix's library, likely because 
   it appeals across age groups and cultures — a smart strategy 
   for a global platform seeking maximum audience reach.

5. Average Movie Duration: ~106 minutes (~1hr 46min), consistent 
   with standard theatrical film lengths, suggesting Netflix 
   mirrors traditional cinema in movie length expectations.
